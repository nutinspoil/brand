What people face

Customers need useful product information, clear categories, and a straightforward way to understand ordering and delivery. When that lives in social-media posts and direct messages, the buying path depends on someone being available to reply.

A storefront makes the catalogue and the ordering path repeatable.

What a system can do

SAD can plan a storefront around product discovery, product detail, ordering, and delivery information.

  • Product catalogue and category structure
  • Product pages with clear images and descriptions
  • Search and browse experience
  • Cart and checkout requirements
  • Order, delivery, and contact information

The dependency: confirmed, not assumed

Payment, fulfilment, stock handling, and administration depend on the agreed providers and the business process. These are settled during discovery rather than assumed from a template.

The storefront is shaped around what the business can maintain after launch — including who updates products and prices.
