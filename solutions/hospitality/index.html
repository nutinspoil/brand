What people face

Guests need to understand rooms, facilities, location, and booking steps before making an enquiry. That information usually sits in phone calls, WhatsApp threads, and third-party listings with different details.

Staff then repeat the same answers, and prospective guests cannot compare what is actually available.

What a system can do

A clear website can bring property details, room information, facilities, rates, gallery, and contact options together.

  • Room and apartment types with accurate descriptions
  • Facilities, location, and directions
  • Rates or rate guidance the operator can maintain
  • A gallery that represents the property honestly
  • One clear enquiry or booking path

The dependency: confirmed, not assumed

Content, photography, and rates must come from the operator and stay current. Booking, deposits, and payment depend on the operator's process and providers; these are settled in discovery, then written into scope.

A website does not fix an unmaintained rate sheet. The first release should include a realistic way to keep the information accurate.
