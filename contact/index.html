What happens next

Three steps, no funnel.

The reply is written by the person who would do the work.

  1. 01The message arrivesEmmanuel reads the situation and checks whether it is a fit for the practice.
  2. 02The first reply names a next stepThat may be a conversation, a short scope, a system review, or an honest recommendation not to build.
  3. 03Scope before workIf there is a fit, the first release and its exclusions are agreed in writing before engineering starts.
