This site describes Success Above Dreams and its software development practice. It is informational — scope, fees, milestones, and client ownership are confirmed in writing before project work begins.

What this website represents

This website provides accurate information about Success Above Dreams and its digital product capabilities. Browsing it does not constitute a formal commercial contract or guarantee of service availability.

What a project agreement covers

Every client project has a written Scope of Work confirming deliverables, milestones, fee structure, third-party costs (hosting, domains, payment gateways), and complete client ownership upon final handover.

Client ownership and intellectual property

Upon final delivery and settlement, the client owns their custom software deliverables, codebases, and production accounts. Success Above Dreams retains ownership of its underlying brand identity, domain, and reusable architectural tokens.
