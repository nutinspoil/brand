## Status and metadata vocabulary

- Project statuses: `Live`, `Private`, `Ongoing`.
- Verification: `Last verified 13 November 2026` on the two public case studies.
- Case-study metadata: `Location`, `Status`, `Visibility`.
- Project cards: `Situation`, `Users`, `Built`, `Result`; private projects also use `State`.
- Service pages: `What the work can include`, `What it depends on`, `Next in the practice`.
- Solutions pages: `What is actually available`, `Seen in practice`.

## Form and state vocabulary

- Groups: `Required`, `Clarify the situation`, `Optional`.
- Primary action: `Prepare the message`. Secondary: WhatsApp handoff.
- Transcript states: `Nothing drafted yet`, `Incomplete - N of N essentials filled`, `Ready to review`, `Message prepared`.
- Validation: field-level error text linked with `aria-describedby`; a visible form status message after preparation.

## Terminology and consistency questions

- The site alternates between first person (`I`) and collective (`we`). Confirm the intended voice for founder-led copy.
- Article bodies use the abbreviation `SAD` (for example, "How SAD can help"). Confirm whether the abbreviation is approved public terminology.
- `Start a conversation` is used for both the project-brief route and direct WhatsApp contact. Confirm whether the two actions should be distinguished in labels.
- `Capabilities` and `Services` both describe the offer. Confirm which term should lead navigation and page titles.
- Case studies use `site`, `storefront`, `website`, `system`, and `product`. Confirm the preferred noun for each project type.
