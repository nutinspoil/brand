What visitors had to do.

Read as the customer reads it, from the pages that exist now.

The requirement

Customers need a way to discover product categories and review the products presented online.

The project is an e-commerce storefront. Stock, payment, fulfilment and ordering behavior are set by the business, so confirm them before relying on the storefront. No sales or performance figures are published.

What the product does.

Taken from the live navigation, pages and controls.

  • Browse a product grid showing images, names and prices in Ghana cedis.
  • Narrow the catalogue with department, category, brand and in-stock filters, plus a search field and sort control.
  • Open a product page with option selection, quantity stepper, Buy now and Add to cart.
  • Reach cart, sign-in and wishlist entry points from the storefront header.
