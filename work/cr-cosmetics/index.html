From WhatsApp product posts to a working storefront.

CR Cosmetics & Essentials, a beauty and everyday-products retailer in Accra, needed a persistent way for customers to browse products, see prices, choose options, and provide delivery details.

One order, end to end

  1. Browse the catalogue
  2. Compare products and prices
  3. Choose a variant
  4. Add delivery details
  5. Place the order

The first three steps are shown in the captures below. Delivery details and order placement happen inside the cart checkout, which the store keeps private to live customers.

What existed before

Customers depended on WhatsApp status posts and repeated conversations about availability, prices, products, and delivery. Status stories expire after 24 hours and offer no search or archive.

Customers repeatedly asked whether an item was still in stock, what the 250ml size cost, and whether delivery reached their area. Retail staff spent hours a day answering the same questions instead of packing orders.

Who it serves

Retail customers — shoppers across Accra and wider Ghana looking for skincare, cosmetics, hair care, and daily essentials with visible prices and clear delivery terms.

Store operations staff — order intake and packing staff who need structured requests with exact variants, quantities, delivery locations, and contact numbers up front.

Decisions that shaped it

Prices on every card, not behind “DM for price”

Customers can compare options before contacting the store. Visible pricing reduces low-intent enquiries and removes a common source of distrust in social commerce.

Granular filtering for a broad inventory

The shop carries hundreds of products across makeup, skincare, soaps, and accessories. Department and brand filters let customers narrow the catalogue instead of scrolling a feed.

Delivery details collected with the order

Checkout asks for the delivery suburb and phone number together with the item selection, so the store receives an actionable order rather than a second conversation.

Low-bandwidth mobile performance

The catalogue avoids bulky client libraries so it opens quickly on 3G or congested mobile connections across Accra.

Evidence

Captures come directly from the published storefront. Nothing has been embellished.

What people can do now

Customers can browse products, review prices, and provide order and delivery information together. Instead of ten messages per purchase, they pick options and place an order with contact details attached. The business receives a clean, ready-to-pack order.

Revenue totals, conversion percentages, and customer records are not published; they belong to CR Cosmetics & Essentials. Inventory, pricing, and fulfilment are managed directly by the merchant.
