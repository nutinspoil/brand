E-commerce storefront for CR Cosmetics & Essentials.

An online storefront project for a cosmetics, beauty, and everyday products business in Accra. The digital store brought product information and ordering details into one public experience.

01 · Situation

What existed before

Customers previously depended on WhatsApp status posts and repeated conversations about availability, prices, products, and delivery.

Because WhatsApp status stories expire after 24 hours and have no search or archive, customers repeatedly had to ask: “Is this still in stock?”, “How much is the 250ml size?”, and “Can you deliver to East Legon?”. Retail staff spent hours every day answering the same questions manually instead of packaging orders.

02 · Intended Users

Who the product serves

Retail customers: Shoppers across Accra and wider Ghana seeking verified skincare, cosmetics, hair care, and daily essentials with transparent prices and reliable delivery terms.

Store operations staff: Order intake and packing staff who needed structured purchase requests with exact product variants, quantities, delivery locations, and contact phone numbers provided up front.

03 · Requirement

What needed to change

The retailer needed a persistent online catalogue where stock status, prices in Ghana Cedis, and product descriptions were always accessible to anyone with a browser link.

The customer journey needed to collect all delivery and recipient details during checkout so the store team received actionable orders rather than fragmented chat threads.

04 · Work Delivered

What was created

  • Complete responsive online catalogue with department, brand, and category filters.
  • Instant keyword search and sort controls (price, newest, availability).
  • Dedicated product detail pages with volume/shade variants and quantity selection.
  • Unified shopping cart and mobile checkout flow collecting recipient name, phone, and delivery address.
  • Fast-loading image optimization designed for everyday Ghanaian mobile data bundles.
05 · Decisions & Constraints

Important decisions made during the build

1. Public price transparency on every card

In many social-commerce channels, prices are hidden behind "DM for price". We made prices visible directly on the grid to filter out casual tyre-kickers and build buyer trust immediately.

2. Granular filtering for a diverse inventory

Because the shop carries hundreds of SKUs spanning makeup, skincare, soaps, and accessories, customers needed the ability to filter by brand and department simultaneously.

3. Combined delivery specification at order time

Instead of agreeing delivery in a second conversation, the checkout form requires the customer to state their delivery suburb and primary telephone together with their item selection.

4. Technical constraint: Low-bandwidth mobile performance

The catalogue was engineered without bulky client libraries so it opens rapidly even on 3G or congested mobile connections across Accra.

07 · What people can do now

Observable outcome

Customers can now browse products, review prices, and provide order and delivery information together.

Rather than exchanging ten text messages per purchase, buyers view exact stock availability, pick product options, and place an order complete with their contact details. The business receives clean, ready-to-pack orders.

Transparency note: Revenue totals, checkout conversion percentages, and customer contact records are not published because they belong exclusively to CR Cosmetics & Essentials. Product inventory, pricing, and fulfillment are managed directly by the merchant.
