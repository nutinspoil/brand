Case study · Private system

A sale is not closed until the shift reconciles.

Private operational system · Client records withheld · Workflow and system logic shown.

A private counter point-of-sale and shift ledger for retail teams working with cash, Mobile Money, and manual stock.

A private counter POS that keeps cash, Mobile Money, and stock movement in one record, and closes each shift against a ledger instead of reconstructed paper books.

System logic

One sale, recorded once, closed against evidence.

A counter POS with role-restricted manager tools, split payment records, inventory movement, and a shift-closing ledger. The system is built around the order a retail team already follows, not a new one.

Roles

Every role has a boundary.

The system gives each person the actions their job needs, and leaves the rest visible but unavailable.

Cashier

Records sales, splits cash and Mobile Money, sees only the current shift.

Supervisor / accountant

Verifies tender, reviews discrepancies, adjusts stock, and closes the shift.

Owner

Reviews recorded activity, role actions, and the shift ledger without changing the day’s records.

Walkthrough

The counter workflow, step by step.

Each step creates or changes a record. Nothing is reconstructed later from memory.

  1. 01

    Record sale

    Item lines and the tender are recorded at the counter.

  2. 02

    Split cash / Mobile Money

    One transaction keeps two payment records instead of two books.

  3. 03

    Update inventory

    Stock moves when the sale is recorded, not at the end of the week.

  4. 04

    Close shift

    Counted totals are compared with recorded tender.

  5. 05

    Manager reviews

    Discrepancies and role actions are reviewed from the ledger.

Result

What the system makes checkable.

Cash and Mobile Money totals can be reviewed against recorded sales when a shift closes, and role actions leave a record for the owner.
