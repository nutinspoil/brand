The eight stages.

Each stage produces something the next one can be checked against.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    Understand the business, people, current process, goals and constraints.

  2. 02

    Requirements

    Identify essential tasks, information, roles and dependencies.

  3. 03

    Planning

    Agree deliverables, priorities, review points and schedule.

  4. 04

    UX/UI design

    Review the structure, content and key interfaces before development.

  5. 05

    Development

    Build against the agreed requirements and share work for review.

  6. 06

    Quality assurance

    Review core journeys, content, links and agreed device layouts.

  7. 07

    Deployment & handover

    Prepare the release and hand over the agreed deliverables and access.

  8. 08

    Ongoing support

    Arrange maintenance or future improvements separately where agreed.

What we insist on.

A defined first release

Not every possible capability belongs in the first build. The essential tasks are agreed, confirmed where they depend on outside services, and released.

Written scope

Project scope, fees, schedule, responsibilities, ownership, hosting and support are confirmed in a separate written agreement before work begins.

The people who will use it

Requirements come from the staff who handle the process as well as the people responsible for the business — the devices, the records and the conditions it runs in.
