The eight stages of the system.

Understand → Define → Plan → Design → Build → Review → Handover → Continue

01

Understand

Grounding the project in real users, physical conditions, and operational truth.

What happens
We investigate the current situation, who touches the task, what is slow or broken, and what environmental constraints exist (devices, connectivity, staff habits).
Client contributes
Candid context about current operations, staff difficulties, customer complaints, and past attempts at fixing the problem.
What is produced
A synthesized problem statement and user persona matrix detailing true daily constraints.
Decision made
Confirmation that digital intervention is justified and whether an off-the-shelf or custom approach is needed.
02

Define

Separating the essential first release from future wishlists.

What happens
We establish the core capabilities required to make the first version successful, eliminating distractions that add cost without adding immediate utility.
Client contributes
Prioritisation of essential business outcomes vs secondary conveniences.
What is produced
A written Scope of Work outlining deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, and definitions of done.
Decision made
Mutual agreement on the exact boundary of Version 1.0.
03

Plan

Mapping architectural foundations, milestones, and integration points.

What happens
We architect data schemas, confirm third-party requirements (payment providers, hosting, SMS gateways), and structure build milestones.
Client contributes
Access to credentials, domain registers, product inventory records, and key stakeholder calendars for review points.
What is produced
Technical architecture blueprint, milestones schedule, and integration dependency map.
Decision made
Technical stack selection and deployment architecture finalized.
04

Design

Shaping ergonomic interfaces, information hierarchy, and user paths.

What happens
We design user journeys, layout screens, test typographic legibility, and model interaction states for both desktop and mobile.
Client contributes
Feedback on content accuracy, brand tone, and simulated staff usability.
What is produced
Interactive interface prototypes, content templates, and responsive design systems.
Decision made
Approval of screens, layout structures, and user flow before code execution.
05

Build

Engineering clean, semantic, high-performance software with direct lead involvement.

What happens
Frontend, backend, database pipelines, and APIs are coded against agreed specifications, with regular staging links provided.
Client contributes
Reviewing working increments on staging environments and supplying final verified content assets.
What is produced
Production-ready code repository, functional database schema, and staging deployment.
Decision made
Validation of functional components against the agreed scope.
06

Review

End-to-end quality assurance, edge-case validation, and device testing.

What happens
We verify every core user journey, cross-browser compatibility, payment flows, error handling, accessibility, and mobile network responsiveness.
Client contributes
Staff user-acceptance testing (UAT) in the physical store, desk, or property location.
What is produced
QA punch list, performance audit scores, and resolution of edge-case bugs.
Decision made
Formal client sign-off for live public deployment.
07

Handover

Full client ownership of code, accounts, domain assets, and operational guides.

What happens
Production launch on client hosting, SSL configuration, staff walkthrough, and delivery of operational documentation.
Client contributes
Designation of account administrator and staff attendance at the handover walk-through.
What is produced
Live digital product, source repositories, administrator credentials, and written operating reference guide.
Decision made
Operational transition to full client ownership.
08

Continue

Long-term stability, agreed maintenance, and evidence-led second releases.

What happens
Ongoing monitoring, security patches, framework updates, and post-launch review to evaluate what future capabilities earn a place.
Client contributes
Reporting real-world user feedback and identifying upcoming operational pivots.
What is produced
Maintenance logs, quarterly performance health checks, and Version 2 roadmap notes.
Decision made
Deciding when the product warrants its next disciplined iteration.

What we insist on.

Three operational rules that keep projects on time and eliminate expensive surprises.

A defined first release

Not every possible feature belongs in Version 1.0. We ruthlessly protect the first build by focusing on the core outcome. Once users are using the product, we make informed decisions about what to build next.

Written agreements and scope

Scope, deliverables, timeline, milestones, costs, and client ownership are established in writing before work begins. No hidden retainers, surprise fees, or lock-in clauses.

Read our terms of work ↗

Real user conditions

We never design solely for high-end flagship computers in air-conditioned design studios. Requirements reflect the actual phones, network speeds, cash handling practices, and ambient conditions the system will live in.
