Service · 06

The same work, recorded away from the desk.

An installable or device-focused application for work that happens at a counter, in the field, or across several devices.

Discuss a cross-device product→ See all services→

Best for

Teams whose work moves between phone, tablet, and desktop, or whose connectivity cannot be assumed.

TestingTechnical experiment Offline-Resilient Register Engine Under technical and performance testing. Not a released product.

Scope

What the service covers.

Work recorded across devices, including away from a desk.

What gets built

  • ✓Device-appropriate interfaces and controls
  • ✓Offline-aware states where the risk requires them
  • ✓A shared data model across devices
  • ✓Installation, updates, and handover notes

What changes

  • The same work is recorded across devices without re-entry.
  • Staff can continue with a clear state when the network drops.
  • A second device does not mean a second record.

Proof

Relevant work, with its boundary.

The nearest public evidence for this service. Private and prototype work is labelled as such.

Evidence boundary

Public work is captured from the live product. Private systems show workflow and roles with client records withheld. Prototypes are labelled as tests, not releases.
