When this is the right form

When the counter runs on handwritten receipts, verbal check-ins, or a register that cannot record how a customer actually paid. The case for a POS exists when the drawer, the payment statement, and the shelf need to agree by the end of the day.

What the work can include

  • Sales and transaction workflows
  • Product and pricing requirements
  • Staff roles and permissions
  • Receipts and reporting needs
  • Inventory or branch functions when confirmed in scope

What it depends on

Every implementation has its own feature set. Inventory, barcode scanning, refunds, offline operation, suppliers, branches, and payment integrations are confirmed project by project. The sale, staff permissions, and end-of-day record are mapped before optional features are added.

Evidence

A private retail register and operations system covers split cash and Mobile Money tender, stock decrement on sale completion, manager-restricted voids, and blind shift reconciliation.

Client interfaces and transaction databases are withheld. The operational systems case study documents the verified logic without exposing client records.
