When this is the right form

When products are already selling through messages, comments, or in person, and the catalogue needs a public home that can hold many items without a person answering every question. A storefront makes browsing and ordering repeatable.

What the work can include

  • Product catalogue structure
  • Category and product page planning
  • Search and browse experience
  • Cart and checkout requirements
  • Order and delivery information planning

What it depends on

Payment, delivery, stock, and administration depend on the agreed platform and providers. These details are confirmed during discovery; the storefront is shaped around what the business can realistically maintain after launch.

Evidence

CR Cosmetics & Essentials is a live SAD storefront for a beauty and everyday-products business. The catalogue, categories, and product pages are public.

Payment and delivery arrangements belong to the business and its providers. SAD does not publish sales or order volumes.
