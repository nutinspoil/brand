When this is the right form

When a product is live but has drifted: content is stale, a dependency is unsupported, or a small change keeps being postponed because no one owns it. Maintenance is the right form when the product is worth keeping, not replacing.

What the work can include

  • Bug and issue review
  • Content and catalogue updates
  • Small improvements by agreement
  • Performance and compatibility review
  • Support arrangements defined in scope

What it depends on

The work depends on the current system, access, and technical condition. Scope and support arrangements are confirmed first. Fixes that reduce risk or unblock essential work are prioritised before cosmetic changes.

Evidence

Support work starts with a review of the current product, access, dependencies, and desired outcome. No maintenance arrangement is sold before that review.
