Service · 04

Requests and approvals out of the chat thread.

A role-scoped operational system for work that currently moves through messages, spreadsheets, and memory.

Discuss an operations system→ See all services→

Best for

Teams coordinating requests or approvals between front staff, accounts, managers, and owners.

PrivateCustom operational software Multi-Role Business Operations Requests, approvals, and their decisions can be followed in one recorded flow. Client outcomes are not published.

Scope

What the service covers.

Requests, approvals, roles, and records in one flow.

What gets built

  • ✓Defined request and approval states
  • ✓Role permissions and visible boundaries
  • ✓Recorded decisions and history
  • ✓Views for the people who act on the record

What changes

  • A request has an owner and a state.
  • Approvals leave a record instead of disappearing in a thread.
  • Managers can review the flow without reconstructing it.
