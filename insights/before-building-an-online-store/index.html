What to decide before building an online store

An online store depends on more than product pages. Planning the catalogue, fulfilment and customer communication helps define the project.

Start a conversation ↗All guides ↗
Subject
E-commerce
Written for
Owners and staff

Plan the customer journey.

A store succeeds when the route from product discovery to delivery is clear.

Map the route from product discovery through delivery. Note who manages stock, fulfilment, payment and customer updates.

1Map the product path

  • How products will be grouped and described
  • How availability and prices will be maintained
  • Which payment providers are appropriate
  • How delivery, collection and returns work
  • Who will manage orders and customer questions

2Choose launch essentials

Start with product pages, checkout and order confirmation. Add promotions or complex stock automation only when the process is clear.

3How SAD can help

SAD can scope the catalogue, checkout and order flow together, including which payment, stock and delivery providers need to connect.

One caution.

Do not assume

Payment and delivery options depend on providers and business processes. Confirm them before describing them as available features.
