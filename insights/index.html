Themes across our thinking.

The recurring principles we apply when evaluating any new software initiative.

Starting something new

Identify the minimal viable surface that validates customer demand without burning resources on speculative features.

Designing for real users

Design around the real lighting, device sizes, internet data bundles, and physical pressure experienced by staff and customers.

Making digital work responsible

Respect user privacy, avoid manipulative dark patterns, deliver accessible semantic code, and provide full client ownership.
