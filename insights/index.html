Themes across the thinking

Start smaller than feels comfortable

Identify the smallest release that proves demand or fixes the core friction. Speculative features are the most expensive part of a build.

Design for the real environment

Real lighting, phone sizes, data bundles, queue pressure, and physical working conditions decide whether a product is usable. Assume those, not ideal ones.

Make digital work responsible

Respect privacy, avoid manipulative patterns, write accessible markup, and hand over ownership. A product that traps its client is not a good product.
